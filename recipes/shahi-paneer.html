Shahi-Paneer

Home

Shahi paneer is a preparation of paneer in a creamy curry sauce. Native to the Indian subcontinent, it has a thick gravy with cream, tomatoes and Indian spices

Ingredients

  • 250g paneer (cubed)
  • 2 onions (finely chopped)
  • 2 tomatoes (pureed)
  • 10-12 cashew nuts (soaked)
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 tbsp butter or oil
  • 1/2 cup fresh cream
  • 1 tsp red chili powder
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • Salt to taste
  • Fresh coriander for garnish

Steps

  1. Heat butter or oil in a pan.
  2. Sauté onions until golden brown.
  3. Add ginger-garlic paste and cook for one minute.
  4. Add tomato puree and cook until the oil separates.
  5. Blend the cooked mixture with soaked cashews into a smooth paste.
  6. Return the gravy to the pan and add all the spices.
  7. Mix in the fresh cream.
  8. Add paneer cubes and cook for 3–4 minutes.
  9. Garnish with coriander and serve hot with naan or rice.
