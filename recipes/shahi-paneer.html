Shahi-Paneer

Home

Shahi paneer is a preparation of paneer in a creamy curry sauce. Native to the Indian subcontinent, it has a thick gravy with cream, tomatoes and Indian spices

Ingredients

    Paneer: 200–500g, cubedBase (Gravy): Onions, tomatoes (often used sparingly), and soaked cashew nuts (or almonds) blended into a smooth pasteAromatics: Ginger, garlic, and green chiliesWhole Spices: Cumin seeds, bay leaf, cardamom, cloves, and cinnamonPowdered Spices: Red chili/Kashmiri mirch, coriander powder, turmeric, and garam masalaRichness & Sweetness: Ghee or butter, fresh heavy cream, a pinch of sugar, and saffronFinishing Touches: Kasoori methi (dried fenugreek leaves) and a splash of rose water or kewra water

Steps

    Make the Gravy Base: Heat 1 tbsp oil in a pan. Add the chopped onions, ginger, and garlic, and sauté until translucent. Add the soaked cashews and chopped tomatoes. Cook until the tomatoes are soft and mushy. Turn off the heat and let it cool completely.Blend and Strain: Transfer the cooled mixture to a blender and blend into a very smooth paste. For a silky, restaurant-style texture, strain the puree through a fine-mesh sieve.Sauté Whole Spices: In a wide pan or kadhai, heat 2 tbsp butter and 1 tbsp oil. Add the bay leaf, cinnamon stick, and green cardamoms. Sauté for a minute until fragrant.Spice the Gravy: Lower the heat and add turmeric, red chili powder, and coriander powder. Stir quickly so they do not burn.Combine: Pour in the smooth tomato-onion puree. Add salt and sugar. Simmer on medium heat for 5-7 minutes until the gravy thickens and you see oil separating at the edges.Add Cream and Paneer: Stir in the heavy cream and crushed kasoori methi. Gently drop in the paneer cubes. Simmer on low for 3-4 minutes so the paneer absorbs the flavors. Do not overcook, or the paneer will become rubbery.Serve: Discard the whole spices (bay leaf/cinnamon), top with a swirl of cream, and serve hot with naan, roti, or jeera rice
