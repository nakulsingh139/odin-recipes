Egg curry is a globally popular, protein-rich dish featuring hard-boiled eggs simmered in a spiced, aromatic gravy

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 4 boiled eggs
  • 2 onions (finely chopped)
  • 2 tomatoes (pureed)
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp red chili powder
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • Salt to taste
  • 2 cups water
  • Fresh coriander for garnish

Steps

  1. Heat oil in a pan and lightly fry the boiled eggs.
  2. Remove the eggs and set them aside.
  3. Sauté onions until golden brown.
  4. Add ginger-garlic paste and cook for one minute.
  5. Add tomato puree and all the spices.
  6. Cook until the oil separates from the masala.
  7. Add water and bring the gravy to a boil.
  8. Add the fried eggs and simmer for 5 minutes.
  9. Garnish with coriander and serve hot with rice or roti.
