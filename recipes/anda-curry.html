Egg curry is a globally popular, protein-rich dish featuring hard-boiled eggs simmered in a spiced, aromatic gravy

Ingredients

    Boiled eggs: 4 to 5 (peeled and fork-pricked) Oil: 2 tablespoons Onion: 1¼ cups (finely chopped or blended into a paste) Tomatoes: 1 cup (finely chopped or pureed) Ginger-garlic paste: 1 teaspoon Spices: ½ tsp turmeric, 1 tsp red chili powder, 1 tsp coriander powder, ½ tsp salt Water: 1 to 1¼ cups

Steps

    Fry eggs: Heat 1 tablespoon of oil in a pan, add a pinch of turmeric and chili powder, and fry the boiled eggs for 2 minutes until golden. Remove and set aside. Make base: Add the remaining oil to the pan. Cook the chopped onions until light brown. Add ginger-garlic paste and cook for 1 minute. Add tomatoes and spices: Add the tomato puree and dry spice powders. Cook until the oil separates from the mix. Simmer gravy: Pour in the water and bring the sauce to a gentle boil. Combine: Add the fried eggs into the hot gravy, cover, and simmer on low heat for 5 minutes. Serve warm with fresh coriander leaves
